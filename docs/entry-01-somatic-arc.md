# Entry 1: Somatic Arc

## Intent

This entry covers J1 through J4 as one continuous body-based progression. The body stays present the whole time, but what counts as the body gradually changes:

- J1: jagged ignition in chest, throat, and head
- J2: brighter full-body self-sustaining resonance
- J3: cooler placid spread and broad laminar flow
- J4: nearly uniform, low-preference luminous equilibrium

## Time Tags

- `jhana 1 (0:00-0:06)`
- `jhana 2 (0:06-0:12)`
- `jhana 3 (0:12-0:18)`
- `jhana 4 (0:18-0:24)`

## Visual Language

- The avatar is intentionally schematic rather than anatomical, so it reads as an energy body and not a medical model.
- Internal dots, depth contours, and glow gradients stand in for packets of sensation distributed through volume rather than just skin.
- Flow lines show how attention and affect travel through that volume.
- Tactile marks change by segment: J1 has short prickling strokes, J2 has more synchronized whole-body activity, J3 broadens into pressure-like ellipses, and J4 keeps only quiet equilibrium traces.
- Warm gold and ember hues mark energized, sticky, rapturous phases.
- Cooler cyan and pale silver tones mark placidity and equanimity.

## Technical Model

- Eight coupled oscillator nodes are placed through the body and aura.
- Each jhana segment changes node weights, coherence, turbulence, pulse rate, and symmetry.
- A segment envelope layer adds attack, sustain, and settling behavior so each phase has its own temporal shape rather than only interpolating static parameters.
- A pseudo-depth pass clips nested contours and highlights inside the avatar to make the body read as a rounded field volume.
- J1 biases head and heart nodes and increases serration.
- J2 redistributes energy across the whole avatar while reducing forcedness.
- J3 lowers pulse rate and raises stillness while preserving coherence.
- J4 compresses the contrast range into a more uniform phase-locked field.

## Why It Fits The Rubric

- It includes an explicit avatar for J1-J4.
- It treats the body as internal volume, not just contour.
- It makes attention dynamics legible through directional flow.
- It makes tactile modality legible through different marks for prickling, pressure, laminar spread, and equilibrium.
- It leans into frequency and coupling language without becoming too literal.
