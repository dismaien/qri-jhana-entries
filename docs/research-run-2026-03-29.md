## Official Brief Details That Matter More Than They First Look

Re-reading the official call for submissions changed the prioritization:

- [QRI call for submissions](https://qri.org/blog/jhana-contest) says the visualization is roughly `75%` of the score and the writeup is `25%`.
- The same page says the judges care about the `phenomenal character` rather than semantic content.
- For J1-J4, the page explicitly requires a body reference and encourages showing `energy body and internal volume`, not merely a skin surface.
- The submission deadline remains `June 4th, 2026, 10PM Pacific Time`.

That means it is not enough for the visuals to be pretty or symbolic. They need to read as:

- bodily
- volumetric
- specific about how attention and pleasure are distributed
- interpretable by a judge who has very recently been in the state

In practice, that pushes this repo away from abstract contour graphics and toward animated tissue, internal glow, and coupled puncta.

## What That Means For This Repo

The first-pass videos were good as sketches, but they were still too clean and streamline-heavy. The next upgrade should make the body feel more alive internally:

- less like contour + ribbons
- more like an organism whose tissue is lighting up, coupling, and reorganizing

That is especially relevant for:

- `Somatic Arc` J1-J4
- `Induction Lantern`

It matters less for `Formless Mirror`, which can stay more sparse and geometric.
