# Study 4: Meditation Atlas

## Intent

This is the less contest-bound part of the repo: a general meditation visualization sketchbook made from reusable motifs.

The current four-part sequence is:

- `0:00-0:06` Grounding Breath
- `0:06-0:12` Body Scan River
- `0:12-0:18` Metta Hearth
- `0:18-0:24` Open Sky

## Why It Exists

- It gives the repo a place to explore meditation visuals without pretending every frame is a precise jhana claim.
- It turns a few visual primitives into reusable pieces you can remix.
- It is a better place to try new moods, palettes, and practice styles before moving anything into a submission piece.

## Reusable Motifs

- breath contours: concentric ellipses around face and chest that pulse with inhalation and exhalation
- body scan bands: broad moving layers that comb sensation through the body
- heart bloom: warm radial expansion from the chest for metta, gratitude, or devotional warmth
- open veil: soft large-scale field structures for open awareness and panoramic attention

## Suggested Uses

- duplicate this entry and tune one segment into a style study
- swap palettes first, then motion profile, then segment pacing
- use it as a landing zone for non-jhana meditation ideas that still belong in the same visual language
