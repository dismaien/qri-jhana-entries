# Judging Cues

Fast internal rubric for reviewing whether the entries are reading clearly.

## Somatic Arc

### Jhana 1

- chest and head should feel like ignition points
- pulses can be sharp, prickly, or corrugated
- energy should not yet feel globally resolved

### Jhana 2

- whole-body coupling should increase
- light should feel more self-propelling and coherent than in J1
- intensity stays high, but effort looks lower

### Jhana 3

- waves should cool and broaden
- local spikes should matter less than laminar spread
- the body should feel content and settled rather than triumphant

### Jhana 4

- near-uniform luminous membrane
- minimal insistence, minimal preference
- equanimity should read as clarity rather than emptiness

## Formless Mirror

### Jhana 5

- image behaves like space rather than body
- openness dominates over objects

### Jhana 6

- reflexivity and self-knowing structure become more obvious
- field should feel nested or mirrored without becoming busy

### Jhana 7

- graspable structure thins out
- the image should resist offering stable handles

### Jhana 8

- almost too slight to depict
- should feel threshold-like and quiet, not merely dim

## Induction Lantern

- breath collection should be visually legible
- spreading pleasant energy should feel invitational, not forceful
- the final section can approach proto-J1, but should not overclaim full absorption
