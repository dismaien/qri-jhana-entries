# Draft Submission: Induction Lantern

This is a draft for the honorable-mention induction-aid category rather than a strict phenomenology replication claim.

## Tag

`induction aid / honorable mention candidate (0:00-0:18)`

## Draft

This piece is meant to function as a soft concentration scaffold. Instead of trying to reproduce a mature jhana with maximum accuracy, it organizes the visual field so attention can collect, spread, and begin to lock in. The structure follows a simple sequence: first narrow the beam of attention at the nostrils and upper chest, then invite pleasant sensation through the torso, then allow the field to sharpen into a proto-jhanic onset.

The avatar remains present because the visual goal is instructional. I want the practitioner to be able to mirror the move in their own body rather than interpret an abstract symbol. A small luminous ring near the upper face acts as a timing cue for collected attention, after which the body volume gradually fills with warmer and more coherent flow. By the final third, the image resembles the earliest phase of the jhana 1 piece but stops short of claiming full absorption.

Technically, it uses the same oscillator engine as the main entries, but with lower intensity, slower pacing, and stronger attentional cues. That continuity is useful because it makes the induction aid feel like an entry point into the same world rather than a totally separate design language.

Before submission, this should be updated with the exact meditation instructions or framing you actually want people to follow.
