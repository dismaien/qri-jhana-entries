# Study 5: Tantric Recognition Atlas

## Intent

This is a post-contest sketchbook entry for nondual Shaiva Tantra and Vijñāna-bhairava-tantra flavored practice motifs. It does not claim to reproduce a specific attainment. It visualizes a practice arc: collect around breath and pause, inhabit the heart, recognize spaciousness within the body, then relax into awareness as an open field.

The four-part sequence is:

- `0:00-0:06` Breath Pause
- `0:06-0:12` Heart Lotus
- `0:12-0:18` Space In The Body
- `0:18-0:24` Recognition Field

## Practice Frame

The piece borrows from the practice language used in Tantra Illuminated and Tantra 112: embodied awareness, breath and pause, heart-centered recognition, mantra or subtle sound as a way of gathering attention, and the VBT-style move of using ordinary perception as the doorway into awareness.

It keeps the body for the first three sections because the practice is not world-denying or body-denying. The body becomes more porous over time, until the final section drops the avatar and treats perception as one continuous field.

Reference points checked while making the motif map:

- Tantra Illuminated's study catalog foregrounds awareness cultivation, the energy body, deity yoga, and mantra science as core facets of the Tantrik path.
- Hareesh.org's VBT verses 22-27 commentary emphasizes pausing at the end of inhale and exhale and experiencing spacious awareness.
- Tantra 112's public app listings frame the app as a modern presentation of the Vijnana-bhairava-tantra's 112 practices.

## Visual Language

- `Breath Pause` uses breath contours and a narrow pause gate between face and upper chest.
- `Heart Lotus` reuses the heart bloom, then adds petal-like forms around the chest center.
- `Space In The Body` opens translucent windows through the avatar so the body reads as spacious and porous.
- `Recognition Field` removes the avatar and uses subtle open veils, lensing lines, and sparse glints to suggest awareness recognizing its own display.

## Technical Model

- It reuses the oscillator engine, body shell, tactile overlays, formless field lines, and segment envelopes from the competition entries.
- It adds four small overlays: pause gate, lotus petals, body-space windows, and recognition glints.
- It is available as `tantric-recognition-atlas` in the app and through the render scripts.
