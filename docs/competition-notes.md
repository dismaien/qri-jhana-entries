# Competition Notes

Paraphrased notes from the official call so the repo stays pointed at the real target.

## Core Format

- Up to 3 videos per participant.
- Each video can be MP4 or MOV and up to 2 minutes long.
- A single video may depict one or many jhanas.
- Each segment should be tagged by time range, e.g. `jhana 1 (0:00-0:50), jhana 2 (0:50-1:12)`.

## Scoring Emphasis

- Visualization is about 75% of the score.
- The written description is about 25% of the score.
- Judges care about phenomenal character more than symbolic content.
- Attention and awareness dynamics matter.
- Frequency, coupling, synchronization, and color-coding are explicitly encouraged.
- J1-J4 need an avatar/body reference.
- J5-J8 are allowed to become much more unconventional and abstract.
- Practical usefulness is a smaller but still relevant factor.

## Prize Structure

- One prize for each jhana from J1 through J8.
- One overall-contribution prize.
- Honorable mentions include best transition sequence and best jhana induction aid.

## Timing

- Submission deadline: June 4, 2026, 10:00 PM Pacific Time.
- Winners expected by June 30, 2026.

## Design Direction Chosen For This Repo

- Split body jhanas and formless jhanas into separate entries so each can optimize for its own grammar.
- Make the body entries explicitly volumetric rather than only surface-based.
- Use a coupled-oscillator visual language to align with the QRI framing without making the pieces look like diagrams.
- Reserve the third entry for induction utility, since that category asks for usefulness rather than strict technical accuracy.

## Research-Aligned Render Levers

Based on QRI's own visualization writing, the most actionable knobs for this repo are:

- temporal frequency as a proxy for arousal
- symmetry / consonance as a proxy for pleasantness
- soft vs harsh ADSR envelopes for how a pulse feels
- tactile modalities such as waves, prickles, and broad pressure blooms
- field-line behavior driven by weighted resonant modes rather than a literal spotlight metaphor
