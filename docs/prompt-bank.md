# Prompt Bank

These are starter prompts for external tools after we already have a target look in mind.

## General Rules

- Use one jhana per prompt unless the model is specifically good at transitions.
- Keep subject count low.
- Anchor body jhanas with a clear seated figure or energy-body silhouette.
- Ask for motion grammar, not just appearance.
- Mention whether brightness should be corrugated, placid, spacious, recursive, thinning, or almost absent.

## Animation / motion add-ons

Append phrases like these when using video tools:

- `the motion should feel phase-locked, coherent, and internally motivated`
- `preserve the exact pose and camera framing; only animate the internal energy field`
- `keep the scene structurally stable while the light field evolves`
- `avoid cinematic action; prioritize contemplative continuity`
- `the transition should feel like a reduction of effort rather than a cut`

## Negative Steering

If a model keeps going cheesy or generic, add:

- `avoid fantasy armor, chakras iconography, mandalas, sacred-geometry wallpaper, stock yoga imagery`
- `avoid smiling face emphasis and avoid theatrical dance motion`
- `avoid generic psychedelic rainbow overload`
- `avoid glossy sci-fi UI overlays unless explicitly wanted`
