# Creative Motif Kit

Quick map of the new reusable pieces in the renderer.

## Motifs

- `drawBreathContours(...)`
  Good for breath following, shamatha, settling, and concentration ramps.

- `drawBodyScanBands(...)`
  Good for body scans, somatic sweep practices, and gradual widening of interoception.

- `drawHeartBloom(...)`
  Good for metta, gratitude, devotional warmth, and chest-centered affective practices.

- `drawOpenVeil(...)`
  Good for open awareness, panoramic attention, and less object-centered practice states.

## Practical Remix Notes

- If a study is too busy, reduce fireflies first and only then reduce flow lines.
- If a study feels too abstract, reintroduce the body shell or stronger node weighting before changing palette.
- If a study feels too decorative, ask what tactile or attentional variable each layer is carrying.
- If a study feels too literal, widen the backdrop structure and lower beam strength.

## Good Next Experiments

- a sleep-onset or hypnagogic study
- a grief or tenderness piece based on `drawHeartBloom(...)`
- a secular breath-coherence visual with slower pacing
- a nondual / open-awareness study built from `drawOpenVeil(...)` plus lighter field lines

## Lab Mode Pairings

- `Settling` + `drawBreathContours(...)`
  Good for gentler shamatha and soft breath following.

- `Warmth` + `drawHeartBloom(...)`
  Good for metta, gratitude, or devotional pieces.

- `Vastness` + `drawOpenVeil(...)`
  Good for open-awareness, sky-like, or nondual studies.

- `Electric` + body-based entries
  Good for sharper concentration ramps or energized somatic work.
